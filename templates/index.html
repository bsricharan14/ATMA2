Welcome to the Automatic Timetable Management System

This system helps institutions efficiently manage class schedules, prevent conflicts, and optimize resource utilization.

Get Started
How To Use

Creating Semesters: Start by adding the semester details. After creation, you will be prompted to add classes (rooms) and courses. You can add these details manually or upload a CSV file.
Note: Time values should be a multiple of the slot duration defined during semester creation.

CSV Format:
The CSV file should include a header row.

Example CSV For Classes:
class_name,class_capacity
Room A,120

Example CSV For Courses:
course_name,num_students,max_minutes_per_week,max_minutes_per_day,min_minutes_per_day,course_abbr
Math 101,30,180,60,60,MTH101

Managing Semesters:
Once your semester is created, it will appear in the management list. You have options to view the timetable, generate a new timetable, or delete the semester (which removes all related data).
Note: When you click on "View Timetable" immediately after creating a semester, the timetable may not be visible because it has not yet been generated. Make sure to click on "Generate Timetable" to create the schedule before viewing.

Timetable Generation:
Click "Generate Timetable" to automatically create a conflict-free schedule based on your defined classes, courses, and breaks. You will then be redirected to the timetable view page.

View & Filter:
On the timetable view page, filter the schedule by room or course to examine specific details.
